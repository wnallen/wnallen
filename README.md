### 🌐 The Legal Hackers Movement

In April 2012, I co-organized a first-of-its kind **Legal Hackathon**—an event that seeded what became **Legal Hackers**, a global movement that has grown to over 130 chapters worldwide. We bring together lawyers, policymakers, designers, technologists, and academics to explore and build creative solutions at the intersection of law and technology.

The founding mission still guides my work:

> *"The goal is to morph and evolve the law on one hand to better serve technologists, enterprises and society, but also harness technology so that lawyers can better service their clients."*
